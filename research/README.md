# Gifted — SEM research

Pre-launch market research and SEM viability analysis.

## Strategy

Profitable RoAS-led SEM at **$2 fixed margin**, premium positioning, overflow + underserved niches, global scope.

## Files

- **`02-niche-heatmap-findings.md`** ← **start here**. The answer to "where can SEM actually be profitable?"
- `01-findings-pre-launch-tooling.md` — earlier write-up on Reloadly catalog reality + SEM tooling needs
- `inventory-matrix.csv` — every (brand × country) row from Reloadly catalog
- `scoring/cells-base.csv` — 1,118 (country × brand) base cells with structural metadata
- `scoring/cells-scored-v3.csv` — full scoring + verdicts (the canonical output)
- `raw/README.md` — pointer to local raw catalog dump

## Quick answer

At strict $2 margin SEM-only: ~$1.9k/yr realistic. Concentrated in Finland.

With SEO landing pages + email loop: ~$30–50k/yr at modest scale, expanding into UAE/Saudi (Arabic), Poland, Greece.

Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Switzerland and most of CEE are inventory-blocked or math-blocked. The "underserved Nordic" thesis does not survive contact with Reloadly's catalog.
